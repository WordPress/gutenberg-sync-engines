# Reporting Security Issues

The Core team and WordPress community take security bugs seriously. We
appreciate your efforts to responsibly disclose your findings, and will
make every effort to acknowledge your contributions.

To report a security issue, please visit the
[WordPress HackerOne](https://hackerone.com/wordpress) program.

How the plugin decides who may join a session, what it checks on every
edit, and what outside processes are trusted with is described in
[docs/protocol.md](docs/protocol.md).
